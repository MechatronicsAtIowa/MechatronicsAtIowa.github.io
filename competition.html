ROBOTS

The lineup

Different roles,
One team

The exact rules change from season to season. These roles are a useful way to understand what the robots do on the field.

HERO

Hero

A larger robot that can do serious damage, but usually needs a lot of power and careful driving.

INFANTRY

Infantry

A fast, mobile robot that moves quickly around the field. It needs to be quick, accurate, and easy to control.

ENGINEER

Engineer

Works with field mechanisms and recovery tasks. A good engineer can keep a match from falling apart.

DRONE

Drone

Uses a flying view to help with scouting and support. It adds another route for approaching the challenge.

SENTRY

Sentry

A defensive robot that watches an area and helps protect important parts of the field, without any human assistance.

BASE

Base + Dart System

These are the fixed objectives. The field layout gives every robot a reason to move and make a plan.
