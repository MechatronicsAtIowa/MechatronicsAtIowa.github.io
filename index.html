WHO ARE WE

About
Mechatronics at Iowa

Mechatronics at Iowa is a new University of Iowa student organization built by students who want to learn more about the various aspects of engineering and build a diverse skillset between mechanical, electrical, and software engineering.

As part of our mission to spread mechatronics education, we compete in varoius compeitions and host events in the STEM community. The team plans on competing in ARC's 3v3 Robotics Competition, as well as the RoboMaster RMUL competition in the future, bringing mechanical design, electronics, and software, together. We also organize workshops and outreach events to promote STEM and mechatronics education.

Our Team

Our team brings together students across mechanical, electrical, software, strategy, and media. With 20+ members already building together, there is a place for everyone to learn, contribute, and grow.

Mechanical

Design the chassis, armor, and mechanisms in CAD, then prototype, test, and improve how every part fits and moves.

Contact Adithya Ashokkumar Mechanical Lead adithya-ashokkumar@uiowa.edu

Electrical + PCB

Plan power, wiring, and sensors while designing custom circuit boards and troubleshooting the robot's electrical systems.

Contact Matthew Brown Electrical Lead matthew-w-brown@uiowa.edu

Software

Write the code connecting driver controls, motors, cameras, and sensors to create the brain of the robot.

Contact August Hagen Software Lead august-hagen@uiowa.edu

Strategy + Media

Study the game, guide design decisions, plan competition strategy, and share the team's work with the community.

Contact Aayush Mistry President aayush-mistry@uiowa.edu
