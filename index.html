Mechanical

+

The mechanical team designs the chassis, armor, and mechanisms in CAD, then figures out how to make the parts fit and move. They prototype, test, and improve designs constantly.

Electrical + PCB

+

The electrical team plans power, wiring, sensors, and designs custom circuit boards. They draw schematics, lay out boards, check clearances, and troubleshoot issues in the robot's wiring.

Software

+

The software team writes the code that connects the driver controls, motors, cameras, and sensors. They create the brain of the robot, making it respond to inputs and physically function.

Strategy + Media

+

The strategy team studies the rules and the game. They make the big descisions on the robot's design and plan what to do. The media team shows other people what we are working on and promotes outreach in the community. You do not need to be an expert writer or designer to help.

DEVELOPMENT

Design and Manufacture

Build the
bots!

The robot starts as a handful of concepts, which we turn into sketches, CAD files, parts, and eventually, a fully functional robot. We compare ideas, check whether parts can actually be made, and work through the hard parts together. We manufacture the metal, write the software, print the plastic, and design the PCBs. We constantly iterate, improve, and refine our designs. Get hands on experience with industry robotics equipment and software.
