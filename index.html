WHY MECHATRONICS

Learn fast,
Make an impact

RoboMasters gives us a reason to build something real with other students. It mixes driving, design, electronics, code, and strategy.

We are still getting started, so there is room to try things, ask questions, and help decide what this team becomes.

THE BUILD

LEarn and Advance

Bring your
expirience,
Build your skills

Mechanical

+

We design the chassis, armor, and mechanisms in CAD, then figure out how to make the parts fit and move. We will make mistakes here, test them, and improve them.

Electrical + PCB

+

We plan power, wiring, sensors, and custom circuit boards. A PCB team can draw schematics, lay out boards, check clearances, and learn how to make a board that is easy to assemble and repair.

Software

+

We write the code that connects the driver controls, motors, cameras, and sensors. The goal is not fancy code; it is a robot that responds the same way every time.

Strategy + Media

+

We study matches, keep notes, plan what to do, and show other people what we are working on. You do not need to be an expert writer or designer to help.

DEVELOPMENT

Design and Manufacture

Build the
bots!

The robot starts as a handful of concepts, which we turn into sketches, CAD files, and parts. We compare ideas, check whether parts can actually be made, and work through the hard parts together. We manufacture the metal, print the plastic, and design the PCBs. We constantly iterate, improve, and refine our designs. Get hands on experience with industry robotics equipment and software.
